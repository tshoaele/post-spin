Post Spin privacy

Draft privacy policy · prepared 12 October 2026 · publisher: Vuja Tsoaela

Post Spin stores drafts, edits, favourites, posted dates, theme settings and any font you load on your device. This version has no accounts, analytics, advertising, AI connection or social posting connection. It does not send your drafts to the publisher.

Imports and backups happen only when you choose a file or save one. Backups contain your drafts and history; protect them as you would your writing. Fonts and theme preferences are stored separately and are not included in the full draft backup.

The Android version bundles its content and does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts or broad storage access. It uses system file pickers for files you choose and your clipboard when you press Copy. It excludes app data from Android cloud backup. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local data.

If the web version is hosted in future, its hosting provider may process request data such as IP addresses to serve the site. A hosted deployment requires a review of that provider’s practices before this policy is final.

Drafts about ASD, ADHD, relationships and life are conversation starters, not clinical advice. Do not put private family or health details in posts unless you intend to share them.

Before publication: add a genuine privacy contact email or contact URL and publish this policy at a publicly accessible URL. No contact address has been invented. Revise this policy and store disclosures before adding AI, tracking, ads, accounts or payments.
